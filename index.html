About GM Network

GM Network is a referral-based product sales platform. You can buy products, refer friends, and earn commissions. Your level increases as you earn more coins.

How It Works

1. Sign up using a friend's referral code.
2. Buy products to unlock your own referral code.
3. Share your code with friends and earn commissions when they buy.

Quick answers about shopping with GM Network.

How do I place an order?

Open Products, select an item, review its details, and follow the checkout and payment instructions shown in your account.

When does my referral code become active?

Your referral code is kept inactive until your first successful purchase, in line with the existing GM Network referral rules.

Where can I check my order status?

Sign in and open your account or order history to review the latest status available for your order.

How can I contact support?

Use the WhatsApp support link in the footer if you need help with an order or account question.
